ANCHOR GOALS · ANDROID APP

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026
In short: Anchor Goals does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All the goals and information you enter stay on your device.

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Anchor Goals ("the app", "we", "our") handles information when you use the application. Anchor Goals is designed to work entirely on your device, without sending your data anywhere.

Information We Collect

Anchor Goals does not collect any personally identifiable information. We do not require account creation or sign-in, and we do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs) of any kind.

Any goals, notes, or preferences you create in the app are stored locally on your device using standard Android storage mechanisms. This information is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Permissions

The app only requests the device permissions strictly necessary for its core features to function (for example, notification permissions to remind you about your goals, if enabled). We do not use these permissions to collect or transmit personal data.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

Because Anchor Goals does not collect any data, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose to third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.

Data Storage and Security

All app data resides locally on your device. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed along with it (unless you have separately backed it up through your device's built-in backup features, which are managed by your device manufacturer or Google, not by us).

Children's Privacy

Anchor Goals does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, because the app does not collect data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
